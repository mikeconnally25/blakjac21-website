Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BLAKJAC21 (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use blakjac21.com and related services (the “Site”), including Kick sign-in, stream games, chat features, and the option to purchase channel points in the store.

We do not sell your information

We may collect sensitive and personal information needed to run the Site (for example account details, technical identifiers, and purchase records). We do not sell your personal information to third parties, data brokers, advertisers, or anyone else.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Site, we may collect:

  • Kick account information when you sign in with Kick (such as Kick user ID, username, and profile picture).
  • Stake username if you choose to link it for stream features, leaderboards, giveaways, or affiliate checks.
  • Technical and security data such as IP address, approximate location (city/region/country when available), user agent / device information, and login timestamps. We use this to protect accounts, enforce bans/alt rules, and keep the Site secure.
  • Activity on the Site such as points balances, reward redemptions, giveaway or game entries, slot requests, chat participation related to Site features, and admin actions where applicable.
  • Cookies and similar technology for sign-in sessions (so you can stay logged in) and short-lived OAuth security state during Kick login.
  • Purchase-related information when you buy points (described below).

Buying points (crypto purchases)

The Site may offer an option to buy channel points with cryptocurrency through the store. When you start a purchase:

  • We create an order linked to your signed-in Kick account (points amount, USD amount, package if selected, and order status).
  • Payment is processed by our payment provider, NOWPayments. You complete checkout on their hosted payment page.
  • We receive payment status updates (IPN) from NOWPayments so we can credit points after a successful payment. We store payment identifiers needed to confirm and reconcile orders.
  • We do not store your full crypto wallet private keys, and we do not sell purchase histories.

NOWPayments processes payment data under its own privacy practices. Review their policy when you pay. Buying points is optional; you can use many Site features without purchasing.

How we use information

  • Operate sign-in, accounts, and session security
  • Run stream tools (store, giveaways, games, leaderboards, requests, chat features)
  • Process and fulfill point purchases and reward redemptions
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, multi-accounting, and banned-user access
  • Respond to support emails and improve Site reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations when required

How we share information

We may share information only as needed to operate the Site, including with:

  • Service providers that host or power the Site (for example hosting, databases/Redis, and NOWPayments for crypto checkout)
  • Kick as part of OAuth sign-in and related platform features
  • Authorities if required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not sell personal information. Service providers are used to run features you request, not to sell your data.

Data retention

We keep account, activity, and purchase records as long as needed to operate the Site, prevent abuse, and maintain accurate points/order history, unless a longer period is required by law. Session cookies are designed to keep you signed in for an extended period and refresh while you continue using the Site; you can sign out at any time.

Your choices

  • Sign out to end your active session cookie on this browser
  • Choose not to link a Stake username (some features may be limited)
  • Choose not to buy points
  • Contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion requests where applicable

The Site is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information (including encrypted sessions and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Site after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.
